# Gesture artwork

`rock.png`, `paper.png` and `scissors.png` are cut out of the "Rock paper scissors banners with hand
gestures" artwork (source file `2202_w023_n001_1896b_p1_1896`) by `tools/make_gesture_icons.py`.

Check the licence of the original artwork before publishing or exhibiting this project. Stock
artwork licences often require credit to the author or the site. Add that credit here and in the
README. The original files (about 13 MB) are not stored in the repository.

To rebuild the icons: `python tools/make_gesture_icons.py <path to the banner .jpg>`
