Rock Paper Scissors AI: redesigned screens

I redid the whole interface from scratch and shortened how you get into the game, so a visitor needs three screens before the first round. The page has 15 screens at the game's real 1280 x 720 size. Below are the main decisions. Look at the screens, press Keep, Change or Drop on each, and write anything you like. Nothing here touches the real game until you say so.

1. Leaderboard firstThe start screen shows who has beaten the AI most, then one big instruction. It is the reason to try, and it updates after every match.
2. Three steps to playStart, type a name, check the camera. The match then starts by itself when a hand is held still. Nothing else stands in the way.
3. Face-off layoutYou on the left, the AI on the right, and the score, countdown and match progress in the middle. The reveal happens where both eyes land.
4. Quick turnaroundA match is 15 rounds, about two minutes. After it, Play again keeps your name, and Next player returns to the leaderboard.
5. A report card at the endA title, a gauge against the one-in-three chance mark, your throw mix and the habits it found.
6. ColoursBlue is you, rose is the AI, on a cool grey screen with a pale cloud behind the AI's hand. Green and red are also used for wins and losses.
