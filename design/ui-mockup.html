Rock Paper Scissors AI: redesigned screens

I redid the whole interface from scratch, including how you get into the game, and drew 19 screens at the game's real 1280 x 720 size. Below are the main decisions. Look at the screens, press Keep, Change or Drop on each, and write anything you like. Nothing here touches the real game until you say so.

1. Face-off layoutYou on the left, the AI on the right, and the score, countdown and match progress in the middle. The countdown becomes huge, and the reveal happens in the middle where both eyes land.
2. Hands-free startNo typing to get in. Quick play (a guest, nothing saved), pick a player from a list, or a new player with an auto-generated nickname like "Swift Fox". Nicknames also keep real names off a public leaderboard.
3. Mode chosen up frontChallenge (the AI hides its thinking until the end) or Learn (see what it expects after every round) is a clear choice at the start, not a hidden key.
4. Scores belong to a matchA match is 15 rounds with a progress track of coloured dots. "Resume scores" and running totals are gone. Each profile only remembers what the AI learned about it.
5. A report card at the endA title (Mastermind, Hard to read, Creature of habit, Open book), a gauge against the one-in-three chance mark, your throw mix and the habits it found.
6. Light, with dark where it helpsLight screens, but the move tiles and the AI's panel stay dark so the hand artwork stands out. The label "Markov AI" becomes "Adaptive AI", since it now blends four predictors.
